## Carousel — The part of weight loss nobody talks about

1. **If weight comes back, did you fail?** Maybe the plan ended too soon.
2. **The setup:** A 68-week study, then a year without treatment. Adults with overweight or obesity were followed after treatment and lifestyle support stopped.
3. **The number:** 11.6 percentage points. The extension analysis reported average regain of prior weight loss by week 120.
4. **The meaning:** Not a character test. The authors discuss obesity as a chronic condition.
5. **The plan:** Maintenance needs a plan too. Support, monitoring, habits and an appropriate next phase belong in the conversation.

Caption: The scale is not a character test. Research shows why long-term support and a planned maintenance phase matter.

## Static — Lean mass is not the same as muscle mass

On-image copy: **Lean mass ≠ muscle mass**. A DEXA scan's “lean mass” can include muscle, organs, bone, fluids and water in fat tissue. The better question: what happened to strength and function?

Caption: Lean mass and muscle mass are related, but not interchangeable. One scan is useful. It is not the whole story.
